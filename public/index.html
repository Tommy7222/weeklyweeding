The Difference

Why Hand Weeding Wins

Most crews are in and out in fifteen minutes. Here's what that actually costs you.

🔪

Weed Whacking

Cuts the top · Root stays

A trimmer takes the leaf off and leaves the root system completely intact. Weeds can be visibly back within a few days — often thicker, because cutting triggers regrowth. It also scalps mulch, nicks the base of your shrubs, and throws debris across the bed.

☠️

Chemical Spray

Keep kids & pets off it

Read the back of almost any herbicide bottle and it tells you to keep people and pets off the treated area until it dries. That's reason enough for us. On top of that, spray doesn't know a weed from the hosta beside it — drift damages ornamentals, and you're left with brown dead growth that still has to be pulled by hand anyway.

🌿

Hand Weeding

Root and all · Nothing left behind

We pull each weed with the root attached, so it doesn't come back from what's left in the ground. Nothing is sprayed, nothing is cut, and nothing touches your plants that shouldn't. Safe around kids, pets, and pollinators — and the bed looks finished the moment we leave.

It takes longer. That's the point. Pulling a dandelion properly means getting the whole taproot — snap it off halfway and it regrows from the piece you left. Multiply that across a full property and you understand why most crews reach for a trimmer instead. We don't. We take the time it actually takes.
